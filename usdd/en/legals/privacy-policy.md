# Privacy Policy

Welcome to usdd.io, a protocol  (the “Interface” or “App”) provided by the USDD Tech Limited (“we”, “our”, or “us”). The Interface provides access to decentralized protocols on the blockchain that support the USDD ecosystem and enable users to interact with various functionalities, including deposit, mint, payback, withdraw and manage digital assets. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access and use the Interface. By using the Interface, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.&#x20;

#### 1. Preface

This Privacy Policy provides the policy regarding the nature, purpose, use, and sharing of personal data or other information collected from the users of the Interface . Please read this carefully as this Privacy Policy is legally binding when you use the Site.

You can reach us with any request relating to this Privacy Policy via contact details provided below.

#### 2. Information  Collected

Information Collected Automatically

The Interface may collect the following types of information automatically:

* Usage Statistics: The Interface uses Google Analytics to collect aggregated data including approximate location, device and browser type. For more information, please visit Google Analytics’ Privacy Policy. To learn more about how to opt-out of Google Analytics’ use of your information, please click [here.](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
* Cookies: analytics cookies including page views and clicks.
* IP Addresses: However, we do not store visitors' IP addresses, including in server logs.

Information You Provide Directly

* Personal Information: Information that can identify you, such as wallet address or email when you voluntarily provide it.
* Blockchain Data: Public blockchain data associated with your wallet address.

Information Not Provided

The Interface does not view wallet private keys, seed phrases, passwords, government-issued IDs, phone numbers, residential addresses, bank card or other financial account details

#### 3. Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Interface uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help us understand user behavior, improve our Interface, and analyze trends. Information collected from cookies may include:

* &#x20;Device information
* Page views
* Button clicks
* Errors encountered

The Interface may interact with  third-party service providers who may also use cookies or similar technologies. You may manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit certain features of the Interface.
